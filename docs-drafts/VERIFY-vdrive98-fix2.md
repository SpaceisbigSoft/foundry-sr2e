## New notes

- **Positioning recorded by the GM's button (replaces the fix1 note):** the button works only on this Combat Turn's card whose author owns one of the two vehicles. For the side the author owns, the GM's client works out the most dice the positioning test could have (the driver as the chase turn finds it: a controller only on the vehicle's side or pointing back at it, else the autopilot; with or without a specialization; + the Control Pool jumped in, at most the skill dice) and the least TN (Handling and the rows the GM's client finds, with the faster vehicle's modifier); the card's own dice faces are recounted against the higher of the card's TN and that TN. A card with more dice than that, or whose faces don't match its dice (a missing or leftover die, a face that isn't 1–6, a die rolled again on anything but a 6), is refused. The GM's dialog shows each side's recounted successes, the dice (and the GM's most) and both TNs. The side the card's author doesn't own makes its positioning test again on the GM's client (with the faster vehicle's modifier, no Control Pool), and that roll counts. A vehicle whose positioning is already recorded this Combat Turn keeps it (the other vehicle of the pair is still recorded).

## Known limits

- Replace "The Light hit's once-guard falls back to this client's memory when the vehicle is a player's and there is neither a started combat nor a viewed scene to record it on." with: "The Light hit's once-guard keeps the newest 200 card ids; an older card applied again would need the GM to confirm it again."
- A player's recounted positioning dice are the faces the player's client wrote on the card (Foundry doesn't check card dice): the GM's client bounds their number and the TN, so a forged card can get at most the successes of all its dice hitting, as a lucky roll would.
- The GM's tick on a pursuer is per combat (a new combat starts with every pursuer ticked).
