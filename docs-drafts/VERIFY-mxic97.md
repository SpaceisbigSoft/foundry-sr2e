#### Matrix IC and cybercombat (v0.97.0, RULES.MXIC_V97)

Switches in `RULES.MXIC_V97` (`rules/config97/mxic.ts`, house-rules window: Magic and the Matrix); the pure functions are `rules/mxic97.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/mxic97.ts`. The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v965` puts v0.96.5 back. Virtual Realities 2.0 (VR2) throughout.

**VERIFY notes:** running a hot deck cool with only 1D6 keeps 1D6 (the book's "reduces Initiative by 1D6" would leave none). The system's "hot" sim is the hot deck on pure DNI; "cold" is a cool deck, or (with Hot ASIST ticked) a hot deck run cool with a keyboard (lead's decision, VR2 p. 85). A defuse that comes up all 1s sets the bomb off (the book says only that the bomb stays primed "unless he rolls all 1's"). An IC's position bonus is always −TN (the GM has no Power choice; the book lets the attacker pick). Lethal black IC's shot comes when the decker is dead (Physical overflow past Body), not at "dying". The reality filter's MPCP −1 lowers the working MPCP everywhere (Hacking Pool, the persona limits, MPCP ÷ 4 for Response Increase). A decker who never answers a choice card adds nothing to the tally until someone (the GM) clicks it.

**Cut / still not built:** construct initiative from the lowest rating (VR2 pp. 51, 120: a construct here has one rating for all its programs, and its initiative already uses it; per-program ratings would be a new data structure); tar baby and tar pit triggering by themselves on every utility use, and the decker's Sensor Test to notice a tar that lost (the GM still acts with the IC; VR2 p. 42); poison scramble reacting to each cybercombat attack on it (scramble is a file's rating here, not an icon to attack); the black IC's dump when it kills the decker and its +2 rating once the icon is down (VR2 p. 47); a reality filter's toggling (half Initiative and +2 TN until the next turn, VR2 p. 84: the filter is a deck field, not toggled in play); the −1D6 Initiative for talking to the physical world (VR2 p. 120: no such state); an opponent persona's evade ending the decker's parry against it (only the decker's own evade does); re-finding an IC that evaded a decker (needs the Analyze Icon operation, a system operation of the other builder).
