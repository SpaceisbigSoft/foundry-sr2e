#### The decker's life outside a fight (v0.97.0, RULES.MXLIFE_V97)

Switches in `RULES.MXLIFE_V97` (`rules/config97/mxlife.ts`, helpers in `rules/mxlife97.ts`, glue in `applications/mxlife97.ts`); the first choice is the book's way (the default), the other ('v965') puts the v0.96.5 behaviour back. Virtual Realities 2.0 (VR2) and the 1998 otaku errata.
