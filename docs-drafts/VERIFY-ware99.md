#### Cyberware and bioware effects (v0.99.0, RULES.WARE_V99)

Switches in `RULES.WARE_V99` (`rules/config99/ware.ts`, house-rules window: Gear); the pure functions are `rules/ware99.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/ware99.ts` (the install hooks and the melee TN row). The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v980` puts v0.98.0 back. The Unified Rules Revamp has no rule of its own for any of these. No new fields, no flags.

|---|---|---|---|---|

A character who has both gets a sheet warning; the better reflexes count (the stacking rule), a control rig keeps its level (VERIFY: the book only forbids the pair, so the GM decides which one goes) | SSC p. 87; Cybertechnology pp. 98–99 | `WARE_V99.boosted` |

**VERIFY notes:** the readings marked above. The trauma damper's Physical-to-Stun shift in the "Not built" list was already built in v0.96.0 (`HEALTH_V96.traumaDamper`); v0.99.0 only corrects its Stun side. "Better" for orthoskin against plating is this system's measure (the book only says they are incompatible).

**Cut / still not built:**
- Uneven Quickness across a pair of cyberlimbs (+ the difference on every movement and coordination test, Cybertechnology p. 51): not modelled.
- The four-limb average as the character's melee Strength when no limb is marked (Cybertechnology p. 50's first option): the character's Strength stays; leading with a marked arm is the second option, built.
- Leg cyberlimbs on kicks and lifting with several limbs (the average of the limbs used): the attack dialog only offers arms; the GM decides.
- Body enhancement of limb pairs (+1–3 Body per pair, Cybertechnology p. 51) and the multiple-enhancement +10% price: not modelled (enter the Body bonus on the limb's attributes).
- Enhancement nuyen (150,000¥ / 175,000¥ per Strength point; partial limbs 65,000¥ / 120,000¥): not priced; the limb's cost field as entered.
- An existing limb's Essence: a player who had already entered the enhancement's Essence in the limb's own Essence cost pays it twice under 'book' (the sheet row shows "enhancements +x Essence"; take it out of the limb's cost by hand).
- Bioware recovery: the "anything but rest adds the time" and "magic doesn't shorten it" parts are the GM's.
- Trauma damper: +2 TN on tasks causing the wearer pain and −2 TN to resist pain (Shadowtech p. 29): not modelled.
- Dermal sheath and orthoskin (Cybertechnology p. 48): the old "only the armor above" conflict rule (the sheath counts as cyber armor) stays; no refusal.

**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):** the refusals are client-side checks on the player's own character (as the v0.96.0 foci limit): a player who gets around them gains only what the sheet still allows (the better reflexes, only the better of orthoskin and plating). The limb's Strength and Quickness fields and its Essence cost are the player's own item fields as before; the book reading only raises a weak field to the base and adds Essence. Nothing here is read by a GM-side decision against someone else.
