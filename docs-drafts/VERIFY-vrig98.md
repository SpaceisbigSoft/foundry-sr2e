#### Rigging, drones, sensors and electronic warfare (v0.98.0, RULES.VRIG_V98)

Switches in `RULES.VRIG_V98` (`rules/config98/vrig.ts`, house-rules window: Vehicles and rigging); the pure functions are `rules/vrig98.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/vrig98.ts`. The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v971` puts v0.97.1 back; the optional electronic deception rule (`ed`) ships `off`. Rigger 2 throughout (it replaces the Core Rulebook's vehicle rules, Rigger 2 p. 20). The Unified Rules Revamp has no rule of its own for any of these, so they read the same under either source.

**VERIFY notes:** the ECCM Test's net successes come off the jammer's (the book says "each success", its example counts the net). A half-step Flux (after the −0.5 rows) reaches halfway between two rows of the Flux Rating and Range Table (the book says to round to the half but not what a half reaches). The Comprehension TN defaults to 4 ("average"); the GM sets it in the house rules. The protocol emulation module and ECCM gear have no field: their rating is read from the item's name ("Protocol Emulation Module 4", "ECCM 4"), the intruder's ECM is a vehicle's ECM or jammer gear, the network's ECCM the vehicle the deck rides in or ECCM gear. The ECM Test is rolled with each Sensor Test (the book makes one test per jammer and compares it with each scanner). Meaconing's degradation counts on the drones' Pilot driving tests; on their Pilot gunnery not yet.

**Cut / still not built:** the missile's attack test itself waiting for Combat Phase 0 (the card says when it strikes; deferring the test needs a GM-side queue on the combat); continuous lock refusing the gunner's other attacks that turn (the card says so); the ECM Test's Signature on sensor-enhanced gunnery and missile attacks (they keep ECM beyond ECCM: storing the test's result on the scanner would be player-writable, rolling it inside the attack's setup isn't possible there); the Task Pool on EW tests; the hardwired deck's + half Body Flux and vehicle Flux boosts (no electronics-port modification); the range check between intruder and target deck; the +2 network alert after a failed decryption counting on MIJI; intrusion's bogus object Perception Test and interference's takeover (the cards say what happens; the GM plays it); re-connecting a lost carrier (Control Systems Test): here a channel works again as soon as ECCM clears a box; the system channel's effect on indirect fire; robots' own Initiative and Learning Pool; the IVIS Pool; the subscriber list's 2 × rating with only rating affiliated.

**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):** `flags.<system>.vrig98Signal` on a player rigger (their own network's Signal Monitor: written by the GM when an NPC's MIJI hits it, but the player can edit their own actor; it only changes their own drones' and their own tests and commands); `vrig98Chair` (their own Initiative); `vrig98Lock` on their own vehicle (the +2 on their own Driving Tests after a continuous-lock launch); the subscriber list (`system.rigging.subscribed`, as before): it only counts for drones with exactly the rigger's owners (`sameSide`), so a player can't put an NPC's drone in their network's phase or degradation; a vehicle's `ed` rating is a sheet stat like its ECM and Signature. Degradation on any other network is written by a GM: at once on a GM's client, else by the MIJI card's Apply (GM), which re-reads both riggers' ratings, re-checks the interception (the target's drones), counts the card's own dice again, needs the card's author to own the intruder, and applies once. The scene's Flux conditions are a scene flag the GM's tool writes. A GM's intruder isn't refused for a missing interception (the target's drones may be a player's).
