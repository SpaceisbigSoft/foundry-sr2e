**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):** `flags.<system>.vrig98Signal` on a player rigger (their own network's Signal Monitor: written by the GM when an NPC's MIJI hits it, but the player can edit their own actor; it only changes their own drones' and their own tests and commands); `vrig98Chair` (their own Initiative); `vrig98Lock` on their own vehicle (the +2 on their own Driving Tests after a continuous-lock launch); the subscriber list (`system.rigging.subscribed`, as before): it only counts for drones with exactly the rigger's owners (`sameSide`), so a player can't put an NPC's drone in their network's phase or degradation; a vehicle's `ed` rating is a sheet stat like its ECM and Signature (ECM, ECCM, ECD, ED and Sensor count at most 20 in the EW tests). The dice signature of a MIJI card is the operation, channel, both sides, the player's TN modifier and every die face the intruder's test used, in order (a card with faces left over is refused): a fresh roll with the very same faces (1 in 6ⁿ for n dice; only likely with 1–2 dice) is refused as a copy; the player rolls again.

## New notes

- R3: `mijiPlan` (rules/vrig98.ts) and the glue's MIJI values clamp the intruder's deck rating and Flux (`rcdSignal()`), the protocol emulation module, the intruder's ECM (`jammerRating()`, from gear `system.jammer` or a vehicle's ECM), and the target's deck rating and Flux to 0–20 (`EW_MAX`); the regeneration rating written on the monitor (`by.rating`) is the clamped one. Book-sized ratings are unchanged.
- R4: the MIJI Apply replays the intruder's dice from the card and refuses the card when any face is left over or missing; the dice signature is built from the faces the test used. A third key, intruder|target|channel and the Combat Turn (the combat's id and round; outside combat the world time), is recorded with the card id and signature: a second MIJI card by that intruder on that channel of that network in the same turn is refused (`SR2E.VRig98.Miji.oncePerTurn`). The record keeps the newest 90 keys (three per card).

## Known limits

- The record lives on the target network's actor. When the target network is a player rigger's (player vs player), its owner can pre-fill or clear that record: harmless, as they can clear their own Signal Monitor too; only MIJI against their own network is affected.
- Outside combat "once per turn" is per world time: a GM who doesn't advance the clock between scenes sees a second MIJI on the same channel refused until time moves (or a combat starts).
