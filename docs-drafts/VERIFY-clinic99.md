#### Clinics, surgery and ware damage (v0.99.0, RULES.CLINIC_V99)

Switches in `RULES.CLINIC_V99` (`rules/config99/clinic.ts`, house-rules window: Gear); the pure functions are `rules/clinic99.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/clinic99.ts`, the GM's tool "Clinics and ware damage (book)" (`clinic99`). The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v980` puts v0.98.0 back. Sources: Street Samurai Catalog (SSC) Optional Rules pp. 93–99, Shadowtech "Cybertechnology" pp. 36–41, Cybertechnology "Cyberware Rules" pp. 44, 53–54, Core pp. 114–115. Two GM-written world settings (only a GM client writes them): `clinics99` (the clinics found, per character) and `wareDamage99` (the ware damage record, per item). New item flag: `ware99Dmg` on cyberware; new patient flag: `surgeryFee99`. No new fields or types.

**VERIFY notes:** the removal test, the damage levels and repair are in Shadowtech's "Cybertechnology" chapter (pp. 36–41; the vault's page images are missing, so the exact page inside the chapter is not pinned; the brief's "SSC p. 97" is SSC's upgrading rule, which the removal test continues). Delta-grade repair TN: the books give none; × 3 (as beta) here; delta repair time × 4 ("Medical costs and repair times for delta-grade cyberware are quadruple", Cybertechnology p. 44). The graded × 50 / × 200 is applied to the surgery fee line as well as to hospitalization (the book's "doctor's fees and hospitalization are 50 times the rates", and the Medical Costs Table's surgery line "includes cost of surgeon"); Cybertechnology p. 44 also says delta's medical costs are "quadruple" standard, which contradicts × 200: × 200 (the specific rule) is used. A clinic of a better grade does the lesser grades too. Damage "accumulates" (Shadowtech) is read as one step past the worse level; SSC's "damaged twice must be replaced" is not used. Cybertechnology's "one success ignores the damage" for graded ware stays (GEAR_V923.cyberDamage); Shadowtech's "each success lowers it a level" is not used. With no Biotech or Cybertechnology skill the removal test rolls Intelligence at +4 TN (as RULES.CYBERWARE.repair's default). The surgeon's fee is charged only when the surgery card's "pay" box is ticked (the same box as the care). A ware's "destroyed" level written on the item alone counts as Deadly (destroyed has no feedback penalty): only the GM's record (a GM client's damage check or the GM's tool) makes it destroyed.

**Cut / still not built:**
- Light damage's half efficiency covers only the item's own attribute, Reaction and Initiative dice bonuses (the augmentation numbers); other bonuses (smartlink TN, vision magnification, a cyberlimb's Strength, skillwires' rating, armor) still count whole (Moderate+ stops all of them). Smartlinks and skillwires are builder C's; cyberlimbs builder A's.
- Interface modules (the 1 in 36 chance and "virtual" damage to the connected systems) and SSC's system tables by body area (the existing pick at random stays).
- Repair parts and labor are shown, not charged; the Build/Repair modifiers table is not added.
- Readers of `system.damaged` outside the model's augmentation and cyber lists (tweaks, vitals' bioware, move-by-wire, the NPC sheet's chip) don't read the level; the book's check still sets `system.damaged` for Moderate or worse, so they see those.
- "You cannot transfer to a cheaper place for recuperation" (graded ware) is not enforced: the care the player picks stands (at the graded rate when paid).
- The clinic search has no player request button: the player asks the GM, who runs the tool. The contact's test uses no Loyalty or payment rules.
- Bioware removal (Shadowtech: it can't be removed without harming the owner) is left to the GM (the tool lists cyberware only).

**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):**
- `ware99Dmg` on a cyberware item (the owner writes it at a hit): it can only make the damage worse (the level counted is the worse of it and the GM's record; "destroyed" from the item alone counts as Deadly).
- `surgeryFee99` on the patient (the operations whose surgeon's fee was paid): clearing it only charges the player again.
- The "Clear the damage record" card a player's repair posts: it names only the item (the GM's client reads the item, its owner and the record level; the author must own the item's actor; the GM confirms every clear).
