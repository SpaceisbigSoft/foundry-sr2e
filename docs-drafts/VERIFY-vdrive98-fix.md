## Replacement paragraph: "**Accepted (player-writable, own vehicle or own people only):**"

**Accepted (player-writable, own vehicle or own people only):** `stress98` (the owner's own vehicle; a player can lower it, as they can its damage); `autonavOff98` (only the owner's own tests: the row it drops costs them the autonav's dice); `offroadSuspension98` (a vehicle stat, as Speed and Handling already are); `vehicleType98` (read by the GM's Maneuver Scores only on a vehicle no player owns; a player's vehicle counts by its category until the GM picks a type in the tool, kept on the combat; every score row shows the type); `belted98`, `apps98`, `crashCage98` (they only lower crash damage to the people aboard that vehicle; the crash cage's dice count only from a card by the vehicle's owner or a GM); the Crash Test card's `cp98` (a player-authored card, bounded by the vehicle's controller's skill when the controller is on the vehicle's side or its rigging points back at it, else 0, and 30; it only adds dice to the passengers' resistance); a passenger damage entry's `vdrive98Dice` (kept from a GM's card; from a player's only when the entry names the vehicle and the author owns it, at most the crash cage's 6 + that controller's skill). Read GM-side from a player's vehicle and checked there: `currentSpeed` (capped at 1.5 × the Speed again for the score), `controllerUuid` (the driver counts only on the vehicle's side or pointing back), `occupants` (aboard only on the vehicle's side), `chase.quarryUuid` (a pursuer only with a combatant in the combat). Everything the chase decides against someone else (scores, Driver Points, positioning carried over, who is hidden from whom, the vehicle types, the chase turns recorded) is on the combat and written only by a GM's client, under the GM lock; the passenger limit is a check on the passenger's own client (as every action check). Under the book rules the v0.57.0 `system.position` on a vehicle (player-writable) is neither written by the chase turn nor read by gunnery any more.

## New notes

- **A Crash Test card's TN (all Crash Test cards, a plain bug before v0.98.0):** the card's TN modifier of 0 (vehicle damage, a push gone wrong, a Stress system failure) was raised to +2 by its bound; it now rolls with no modifier; a tires card keeps its +n (chat.ts `vehicleCrashTest`). Not switched (it was never the book's or anyone's intent).
- **Positioning recorded by the GM's button:** the GM's dialog lists each side's successes and dice from the card; the side the card's author doesn't own makes its positioning test again on the GM's client (with the faster vehicle's modifier, no Control Pool), and that roll counts. A card from an earlier Combat Turn is refused (its successes were for the turn after it).
- **The GM's override:** a GM's own attack or spell by a passenger above the Driver Points, or at a hidden vehicle, is warned only; inside a player's card re-run on the GM's client the player is judged (refused).
- **Rolling the Maneuver Scores twice in a turn:** the second roll reads the positioning successes the first spent; positioning recorded since then stays for the next turn.

## Known limits

- "Hit a wall" (the GM's tool) still reads the selected vehicle's current speed as it stands (capped at 1.5 × the Speed only when the speed is set); the GM sees it on the card.
- The Light hit's once-guard falls back to this client's memory when the vehicle is a player's and there is neither a started combat nor a viewed scene to record it on.
- A vehicle without a combatant (an empty vehicle not added to the combat) doesn't count as a pursuer: add the chasing vehicles to the combat.
- The GM's pick of a vehicle type is per combat (it starts again by category in a new combat).
