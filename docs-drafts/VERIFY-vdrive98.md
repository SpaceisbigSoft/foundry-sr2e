#### Driving, vehicle combat and chases (v0.98.0, RULES.VDRIVE_V98)

Switches in `RULES.VDRIVE_V98` (`rules/config98/vdrive.ts`, house-rules window: Vehicles and rigging); the pure functions are `rules/vdrive98.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/vdrive98.ts`, the GM's tool "Vehicle chase (book rules, GM)" (`chase98`). The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v971` puts v0.97.1 back. Rigger 2 throughout. Under the Unified Rules Revamp its own chase rules win: `chase`, `passengerPhase` and `gunneryRows` read `v971` there. New vehicle fields: `offroadSuspension98`, `autonavOff98`, `stress98`, `belted98`, `apps98`, `crashCage98`, `vehicleType98` (a new row on the vehicle sheet).

**VERIFY notes:** the Vehicle Points of a vehicle whose type isn't set come from its category (car → car, bike → motorcycle, truck → limousine/van, boat → small motorboat, ship → yacht, rotor → helicopter, fixed-wing and vectored thrust → small airplane, LTA → zeppelin): this system's reading. The part thresholds by part (tires, engine, a fixed mount Moderate; sensors Light; a turret Serious) apply the book's examples (antennas, turrets). The autonav row applies only when a character drives (an autopilot-driven vehicle is the autonav itself); a switched-off autonav adds no dice to Driving or Crash Tests (the book gives the dice to "every autonav system", read as a working one). Stress from exceeding the Speed: +1 per speed change that ends above the Speed while speeding up (the book's example: 250 → 286 with Speed 210 costs one). Relocating rolls the driver's Intelligence (the book's "Perception Test"); an empty vehicle its autopilot rating. NPC drivers giving up after 5 turns is only said on the card. Every spell cast counts as an offensive action for the passenger limit ("shooting weapons or casting spells").

**Cut / still not built:** the Open Test of the Driver Points for NPCs uses no Professional Rating dice (the GM enters the Control Pool dice of each jumped-in rigger in the tool; NPC Professional Rating dice are not added); multiple-vehicle groups rolling once (p. 47); the hiding vehicle's passengers and the pursuer's passengers being unable to act against each other is enforced only for attacks and spells (not for sensors or Matrix actions); the off-road suspension's own −15 / −30 Speed and Handling changes (Rigger 2 p. 125: put into the vehicle's stats when fitted); roll bars (+3 dice, Rigger 2 p. 129); system failures' effects (the GM chooses the system: only the Crash Test is offered); Stress-reducing overhauls (Rigger 2 p. 25); monthly overhead and Stress (Rigger 2 p. 18); deceleration beyond the safe limit (a Body Resistance Test, Rigger 2 p. 22); the optional terrain-based Control Pool limits (Rigger 2 p. 53); a jacked-in datajack driver's Initiative (+1 Reaction is applied to the driving test only, as agreed with the rig-side builder).

**Accepted (player-writable, own vehicle or own people only):** `stress98` (the owner's own vehicle; a player can lower it, as they can its damage); `autonavOff98` (only the owner's own tests: the row it drops costs them the autonav's dice); `offroadSuspension98` and `vehicleType98` (vehicle stats, as Speed, Handling and category already are: the GM's score card shows each vehicle's partial score); `belted98`, `apps98`, `crashCage98` (they only lower crash damage to the people aboard that vehicle); the Crash Test card's `cp98` (a player-authored card, bounded at the rigger's vehicle skill and 30; it only adds dice to the passengers' resistance). Everything the chase decides against someone else (scores, Driver Points, positioning carried over, who is hidden from whom) is on the combat and written only by a GM's client from tests it rolled; the passenger limit is a check on the passenger's own client (as every action check). Under the book rules the v0.57.0 `system.position` on a vehicle (player-writable) is neither written by the chase turn nor read by gunnery any more.
