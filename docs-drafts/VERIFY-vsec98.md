#### Building security and vehicle upkeep (v0.98.0, RULES.VSEC_V98)

Switches in `RULES.VSEC_V98` (`rules/config98/vsec.ts`, house-rules window: Vehicles and rigging); the pure functions are `rules/vsec98.ts`, the Foundry glue `applications/vsec98.ts` (the GM's "Security and vehicle upkeep (book rules, GM)" tool, `vsec98`). The first choice is the book's way (the default); `v971` puts v0.97.1 back. Rigger 2 ("The Security Rigger", "Standard Vehicle Operations", "Vehicle Customization", "Vehicle Design") and the Corporate Security Handbook (CSH, "Gamemaster Information"). **VERIFY notes:** the ECCM table's level 1 and 3 prices (printed 110,000¥ and 350,000¥ in the scan) are read as 10,000¥ and 50,000¥ (the table's Design Points × 100, as for the other levels and the ECM and sensor tables); the military levels' "1.25¥", "7.5¥", "2.5¥" as millions. A table item's price is the table's price for the level it reaches (the book prices systems, not upgrades). Improved suspension's "vehicle's original cost × 1.10 per increment" is read as 10% of the cost per increment. The armour Installation Test's "desired Armor Rating ÷ 3" rounds up, and "Armor value × 8 hours" counts the points added. Thermal baffles for categories the book doesn't list (vectored thrust, other) use the ground vehicles' 5,000¥. The All-terrain Vehicle chassis' 445 Design Points are as printed. The Technical Security Ratings Table's column heads (Alarms interior/exterior, CCTV exterior/interior) are taken literally, though CSH p. 96's level 2 text puts exterior sensors there. A decker resisting rigger combat damage gets no pool and the +2 TN.

**Cut / still not built:** the chassis and power plant tables as a full design automation (power plant rows are per vehicle group with formula cells: the GM types the power plant's and options' Design Points; the Power Plant Table is not in the data); a vehicle built from the calculator (it posts the numbers only); engine customization's price (from the original power plant's cost, unknown here) and its secret failure test; installs needing two skills (remote-control interface: vehicle B/R and Electronics B/R; one skill is rolled, the item's own first); each modification's maximum, CF, Load and Availability; the ultrasound emitter/detector's opposed test against motion sensors; pressure mesh and pads (CSH p. 100); the rigger combat Initiative (decker −2, no Response Increase) applied to the combat tracker; the CCSS limits enforced on firing or commanding (shown on the panel only); the decryption system rated at the Security Level (Rigger 2 p. 77) and the jump into rooms (Rigger 2 p. 78); the CSH's older access TNs (twice the security level, OSL successes; Rigger 2 replaces them); Task Pool dice on the access steps.

**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):** `vsec98Resisted` on a rigger combat loser's actor (the damage cards they have resisted: clearing it only lets them resist the same card again, which hurts only themselves). The rigger decking penalty reads the decker's own VCR rating and their own installed ware's names (a player can already change their own Matrix dice). Everything the GM side decides about a site (Technical Rating, Vigilance Code, levels, recorded access steps) is on the site, and an access step's success comes from the request card the GM posted and the dice of a roll card whose author owns the intruder.

New fields on existing types: none (the site's values are a flag; `vibration` is a new choice of a device's `sense`).
