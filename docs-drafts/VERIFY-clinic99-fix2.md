## Replacement Accepted paragraph

**Accepted (player-writable, own character only):**
- `ware99Dmg` on a cyberware item (the owner writes it at a hit): it can only make the damage worse (the level counted is the worse of it and the GM's record; "destroyed" from the item alone counts as destroyed, no better for the player than Deadly). On ware not installed it is the owner's own until the ware goes in (it does nothing while out).
- `ware99Fix` on a cyberware item (a repair project's effect writes it): a player's write of it clears nothing; at most it makes the lead GM post the clear card for a repair test that checks out, and the GM confirms.
- `surgeryFee99` on the patient (the operations whose surgeon's fee was paid on their own client): clearing it only charges the player again; the GM's client uses its own `surgeryFees99`.
- The grade frozen on the surgeon's own surgery card and operation state (`grade99`, S6): a player can freeze `standard` on their own card. The surgery card's "pay" box off already skips every charge, so this gives no more than the player could already do, and the GM's apply confirm shows the fee and the care's cost.
- The "Clear the damage record" card a player's repair posts: it names only the item and the repair test (the GM's client reads the item, its owner, the record level and the repair test's card; the author must own the item's actor; the GM confirms every clear).

## New notes

- GM write steps of the clinic glue (the tool's saves, the removal, the GM's level setting, the clear, the surgeon's fee) take the GM lock unless they run inside a player's card re-run (`actingAuthor` / `inAuthorRun`); another locked task holding the lock no longer makes them run inline. - `wareDamage99Meta` also keeps, per recorded key, the time of its last rise (the server's clock) and the ware's name and kind when first recorded (at most 2,000; dropped when the key is cleared or its item is gone).
- The GM is whispered every time the damage record or the deleted-ware notes drop an entry to make room (no longer once a session).

## Known limits

- The proof of a repair is whatever the repair card claims (its dice, its typed successes, the item it names); the checks bound it (author, ownership, TN, time, once only), and the GM's confirm, which shows the card's time against the damage's rise, is the control.
- A recorded ware a player deletes keeps its level only on characters that player owns (and the ware's own); moved to a character of another player it keeps none.
- A deleted ware's level goes to every installed ware of the same name and kind on those characters (a character with two identical systems gets it on both).
- A damage level on ware not installed is the owner's own: a player can lower it before the ware goes in.
- The lead GM's whisper for cyberware a player takes out past the refusal comes after the fact; nothing is put back.
