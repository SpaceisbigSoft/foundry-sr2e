## Bugs found while writing

Found by reading the code; each has a check marked **Watch** that shows it live.

1. **A used "Clear the damage record (GM)" card works again later.** `applyClear` (`applications/clinic99.ts:462–480`) writes `clinic99Cleared` on the card (`:476`) but nothing ever reads it (the flag is defined at `:68` and used only there); the card names only the item, not the level or the repair it belongs to. After the ware is damaged again, the GM's click on the **old** card (or on a player's copy of it) offers to clear the **new** level (one confirm, then cleared). Scenario: check 55(e).
2. **The lead GM's sweep re-records a level whose repair is under way, so the ware stays damaged after the repair.** A GM's repair of a character's ware is a project (`documents/index.ts:3318–3320`, `waitsFor('repairs')` is true for every character); `repaired(…, { project: true })` clears the GM's record at once but leaves the item's own `ware99Dmg` until the project ends (`applications/clinic99.ts:194–200`). If Tab G logs in or reloads meanwhile, `sweepLevels` (`:501–512`, run at `ready`, `:533–536`) sees the item's level above the (cleared) record and writes it back; when the project ends and clears the item's flag, the record still holds the level: the ware keeps its damage (and its feedback TN) until a GM clears it by hand. Scenario: check 37.
3. **A project repair can end the damage before (or without) the repair.** (a) The GM's "Clear the damage record" on a player's project repair also wipes the item's own level (`applications/clinic99.ts:475`), so a Light-damaged ware works at full at once though the repair hours haven't passed (the project's own effect was meant to clear it, `documents/index.ts:3318`). (b) When a GM's TN change or a Karma re-roll turns a project repair into a failure, the cancel branch (`documents/index.ts:3297–3300`) only cancels the project: the record cleared at the start (GM repairer) is not put back and, after (a), neither is the item's level, so the ware stays "repaired" (and in the GM-repairer case its owner can now lower the item's own flag and lose the damage for good, because the record no longer holds it). Scenario: check 38.
4. **A second surgery on the same ware charges the surgeon's fee again.** `surgery()` (`documents/index.ts:2389–2404`) doesn't refuse ware that is already installed and gives every roll a new operation id; `chargeSurgeonFee` (`applications/clinic99.ts:237–249`) keys the "once per operation" only by that id, and `#resolveSurgery` calls it whenever this operation's own state isn't installed (`documents/index.ts:2577–2582`), even though the item already is. A double-click on the sheet's Surgery button with both prompts confirmed (or a console call) pays the fee and the care twice — at alpha grade 2 × 275,000¥. Scenario: check 62.
5. **The police check never looks at a carried cyberdeck or focus.** `policeItems` (`applications/kit99.ts:150`) takes only weapon, armor, gear and cyberware, though cyberdecks (`models/index.ts:2043`) and foci (`:1940`) have `legality` and the new `legalCode` too; a restricted deck is skipped even with "Everything they carry" ticked. Scenario: check 54.
6. **The clinic tool has no in-flight guard.** `openClinicTool` (`applications/clinic99.ts:426–443`) can be opened twice (the police tool has `toolBusy`, `applications/kit99.ts:197–203`): two clinic searches roll and post twice for one request; two removal chains on the same ware open together (the second is silently dropped by `removeWare`'s installed check). Scenario: check 62.
7. **Wording (cosmetic).** `public/lang/en.json:21546–21550`: "looks for a alpha-grade shadow clinic", "They know a alpha-grade clinic.", "(needs 1 successes)"; the house-rules row `CLINIC_V99__removal` (`:10015`) names "Cybernetics" while the skill rolled is Cybertechnology (`RULES.CYBERWARE.repair.skills`). Record in 23 and 2.
