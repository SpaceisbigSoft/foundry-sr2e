**What changed.** A world setting `sr2e.rulesSource` (`'book'` is the default; `'urr'`) is shown as "SR2E Book Rules" or "SR2E Unified Rules Revamp". It can be switched in Game Settings (no confirmation) and in the GM panel's Setup tab (a confirmation dialog). Under `'urr'`, `URR_V80_PATCH` (139 rules plus `URR_V80.active`) is laid over the shipped RULES and under the house rules. The patch lives in `rules/config80.ts` and `config80-<area>.ts`, with the pure logic in `urr80-<area>.ts`. The house-rules window marks the 114 rows that the Revamp changes and shows the Revamp's value as their default. **The absolute rule: nothing may change under the Book Rules.**

Sources of truth: the headers of `src/module/rules/config80-*.ts` and the "VERIFY rows" and "Player-guide notes" in the six `<area>-WIRING.md` files. The glue is `URR_V80` / `U80C` in `documents/index.ts`, `applications/chat.ts`, `applications/sheets.ts` and `models/index.ts`. The settings layer is `settings.ts` (`applyHouseRules`, `baseRule`, `sourceChangedRules`, `HouseRulesConfig`) and `applications/setup.ts` (`bindSetupTab`).

Dice faces are ceil((1 − u) × 6): **0.2 → all 5s, 0.5 → all 3s, 0.75 → all 2s (no successes against TN 3 or more, and no disaster), 0.85 → 1s, 0.9 → all 1s (a disaster), 0.1 → a 6 (it explodes: the system rolls every die first, then a wave for the 6s, in order), 0.4 → 4, 0.6 → 3.** Never use a constant below 0.17.
