**Dice.** Faces are ceil((1 − u) × 6): **0.2 → 5, 0.75 → 2 (no success against TN 3 or more, and no disaster), 0.85 → 1, 0.1 → 6 (explodes)**. Never use a constant below 0.17. A mix of 1s and other faces is not a disaster.

**The rules this plan assumes** (check them in 0): `rulesSource === 'book'`, no house rules, so `R.URR_V80.coreKarmaDice` is falsy and `R.CORE_V923.karmaDice === 'book'` (Karma Pool dice are capped at the test's own dice), `R.CORE_V923.threat === 'book'` (an NPC's Threat Rating adds dice), `R.MAGIC_EXTRA.area.radiusFrom === 'magic'` (an area spell's radius is Magic × 1 m), and `ATTACK_STAGING === 'afterFirst'`. If any of these differs, stop and ask.
